# MyBizAI: Stakeholder Overview

## 1. Introduction & Vision

MyBizAI is an ambitious, AI-powered business planning and management platform. Our vision is to create an all-in-one "AI co-pilot" that simplifies and enhances the entire process of building and managing a business, from the initial idea to day-to-day operations.

## 2. Target Audience

MyBizAI is designed for a diverse range of users, including:

*   **Aspiring Entrepreneurs:** Individuals at the very beginning of their business journey.
*   **Startup Teams:** Small to medium-sized teams who need to collaborate effectively.
*   **Business Consultants & Coaches:** Professionals who can use the platform to guide their clients.
*   **Students & Educators:** Those in business and entrepreneurship programs.

## 3. Key Features

The platform is designed with a comprehensive set of features to meet the needs of our target audience:

### Core AI-Powered Tools
*   **Idea Generation:** AI-driven tools to help users brainstorm and validate new business ideas.
*   **Business Plan Editor:** A collaborative, AI-enhanced editor for creating professional business plans.
*   **Market Research & Financial Projections:** AI-powered tools to analyze competitors, understand the market, and create financial forecasts.

### Project & Team Management
*   **Unified Workspace:** A single platform for project tracking, task management, and team communication, eliminating the need for multiple applications.

### User & Account Management
*   **Secure & Easy Access:** Simple and secure login options, including 2-Factor Authentication (2FA).
*   **Subscription Tiers:** Multiple subscription plans (Standard, Pro, Business) to cater to different user needs.

### Marketing & Growth
*   **Public-Facing Presence:** A professional website with a blog, resource library, and community forum to attract and engage users.

## 4. Strategic Roadmap

Our development plan is broken down into three phases:

### High Priority: Core Functionality
Our immediate focus is on building the essential features of the platform:
*   **Core AI Features:** Implementing the AI-powered tools for idea generation, business planning, market research, and financial projections.
*   **Project & Team Management:** Building the foundational tools for collaboration and project tracking.
*   **Subscription & Billing:** Ensuring a seamless and secure payment and subscription management system.

### Medium Priority: User Experience & Growth
Once the core functionality is in place, we will focus on enhancing the user experience and driving growth:
*   **Onboarding & Personalization:** Creating a guided onboarding process and adding features for personalization.
*   **Marketing & Community:** Launching the blog, resource library, and community forum to build a strong user base.

### Low Priority: Long-Term & Foundational
These are important long-term goals that will be addressed after the core platform is established:
*   **Formalize Documentation:** Creating comprehensive guides for both users and developers.
*   **Automated Testing:** Implementing a robust testing strategy to ensure platform stability and reliability.

## 5. Conclusion

MyBizAI is positioned to be a market-leading platform that empowers entrepreneurs and businesses. By combining powerful AI tools with a comprehensive suite of management features, we are creating a unique and valuable solution that will redefine business planning and management.
